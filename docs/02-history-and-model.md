# 02 — History and Operating Model

## The four eras of YFYİ

The program did not stay the same thing for 21 years. It went through four clearly
distinguishable phases, and the phase determines what "winning YFYİ" even means in a
given year.

---

### Era 1 (2005–2013) — The student business-idea competition

**Name in use:** *"Yeni Fikirler Yeni İşler" Girişimcilik Yarışması* / *En İyi Teknolojik
İş Fikri Yarışması*.

**Shape:** A classic competition. Undergraduate/graduate students and recent graduates
submit a technology-based business idea. Applications are screened down through stages;
finalists take ~100 hours of "Entrepreneurship and Business Plan" training; a jury picks
winners at a final ceremony (*final töreni*), typically in **November** at the ODTÜ Culture
and Congress Centre (Kemal Kurdaş Hall).

**Prize structure:** A small number of large cash prizes — the *Elginkan Vakfı Teknoloji
Ödülü / Büyük Ödülü* being the flagship (50k TL in 2007 → 75k TL 2008–2011 → 100k TL from
2012 onwards), paid **in instalments tied to getting to production** (*"üretime geçmeleri
için kademeli ödenmek üzere"*). Winners also got the right to operate as a company inside
ODTÜ TEKNOKENT for 2–3 years. **[C]**

**Categories** appear and multiply over the era:
- 2011–2013: *Genel*, *Bilişim ve Telekomünikasyon*, *Savunma Sanayii*
- 2014: five categories — *Genel*, *Bilişim ve Telekomünikasyon*, *Savunma Sanayii*,
  *Enerji*, *Sağlık*

**Funnel example (2013, 9th edition):** 1,071 entrepreneurs applied with 777 distinct
business ideas → 541 passed stage 1 → 30 reached stage 2 and took ~100 hours of training
→ 11 finalists across 3 categories. Roughly **1% of applicants reached the final**. **[R]**

---

### Era 2 (2014–2016) — Scaling up, splitting the funnel, going to the US

**2014 (10th):** Run *with Teknopark İstanbul*, with **TEB as organisation main sponsor**.
1,353 business-idea applications; 1,244 passed the first stage; 47 reached the last stage;
**21 teams** competed in 5 categories. Total award package advertised at **~2 million TL**.
Introduction of **"ABD Kampı" (US Camp) special prizes** — 25,000 TL each from eight
different sponsors. **[R]**

**2015 (11th):** The program formally splits into two tracks: **[C]**
- **YFYİ IDEA** — for people *without a company*: undergrad/masters/PhD students and
  graduates up to 5 years out. No sub-categories.
- **YFYİ PRO** — for technology companies *0–3 years old*, in four categories:
  *Dayanıklı Tüketim ve Tüketici Elektroniği* (durables & consumer electronics),
  *Bilişim* (computing, with Intel), *Enerji*, *Sağlık*.

23 finalists. Winners get a 2-week US Camp at the **ODTÜ TEKNOKENT T-Jump San Francisco
Centre**. Y Combinator participation was dangled for the PRO track. Microsoft (Azure) and
IBM (Bluemix) added $120k-value cloud-credit prizes. A public vote (*halk oylaması*)
prize was introduced.

**2016 (12th):** Renamed in official copy from *yarışma* (competition) to **Hızlandırma
Programı** (acceleration program). ODTÜ TEKNOKENT GM Mustafa İ. Kızıltaş: *"In 2005 we ran
this as a student business-idea competition; today we run it asking how we can build a
technology company that will operate worldwide. So YFYİ is no longer a competition, it is
an acceleration program."* **[C]**

Three sub-programs are introduced: **[C]**
- **T-IDEA** — pre-company; students and graduates ≤5 years out.
- **T-START** — companies aged 0–4 that have raised <100k TL; focus on commercialisation
  in IT, health, energy, consumer electronics.
- **T-BOOST** — companies with product + team ready for foreign markets; internationalisation.
- **T-BİGG** — teams meeting TÜBİTAK BİGG criteria route into 150,000 TL of TÜBİTAK support.

Bootcamps run by **String Ventures**; Demo Day 15 October 2016; winners get a 4-week camp
at **T-Jump San Francisco**.

---

### Era 3 (2017–2019) — The globalised accelerator

**2017 (13th)** is the structural high-water mark. **[C]**

The pipeline that year:
1. **400+ applications**
2. **50 teams** into a 6-week **pre-accelerator**, delivered by the **UC Berkeley
   Innovation Acceleration Group** using the **Lean Launch** method (the NSF I-Corps
   method used at Stanford and elsewhere), with a Turkey-specific **Design Thinking**
   module added. Led by **Ozan Sönmez** (Managing Director, T-Jump), with UC Berkeley's
   **Mark Searle** (Director, IAG), **Elizabeth Saunders** (Chief of Staff), and trainer
   **Amal Dokhan**. UC Berkeley staff flew to ODTÜ TEKNOKENT twice in two months and
   worked with 70+ entrepreneurs on 50+ ideas.
3. **Bootcamp** at ODTÜ TEKNOKENT, 26–27 May 2017. Day 1: feedback on business-model
   evolution. Day 2: final pitches for US selection.
4. **7 teams → Draper University "Hero Training"**, 7 weeks in San Francisco alongside
   ~40 entrepreneurs from other countries, ending with pitches to **Tim Draper** and other
   investors.
5. **Demo Day**, 14 October 2017, ODTÜ Culture and Congress Centre, hosted by **Metin Uca**.
   22 startups, **3-minute pitches**.
6. **Growth Circuit** (ODTÜ TEKNOKENT's SF acceleration + investment arm, founded 2016)
   selected 7 startups for investment.

**2018 (14th):** 55 teams admitted; **1,000+ hours** of training and mentoring; bootcamps
with **Growth Circuit + UC Berkeley IAG**; 4 teams (WardenLegal, Blok-Z, Sensgreen, Viliks)
took Growth Circuit investment and went to Draper University. Second-stage evaluation
pitches on 5 September 2018 selected **21 teams** for Demo Day (6 October 2018,
ODTÜ KKM, ~1,500 attendees). **[R]**

**2019 (15th):** Applications 5–22 August 2019. The Demo Day was restructured into a
two-day residential **"Inspiration Weekend"** in **Sapanca, 1–2 November 2019**: day 1
inspirational talks from ecosystem leaders, day 2 the Demo Day with **24 startups**
pitching. Two full days of co-location with partners and investors. **[C]**

---

### Era 4 (2020–2025) — Post-pandemic, international partners, December Demo Days

**2020 (16th):** Delivered with **Startup Wise Guys** (Estonia-based accelerator and
early-stage fund) and international mentors. **40 teams admitted → 10 weeks of training
and mentoring → 27 completed → 19 pitched.** Demo Day held **online on Friday 2 April
2021**, live-streamed on YouTube, with panels titled *"Sağlığı Tasarlayanlar"*,
*"Yenilik Yaratanlar"*, *"İş Dünyasına Yön Verenler"*. ODTÜ TEKNOKENT introduced its own
**Uluslararasılaşma Ödülü** (internationalisation award) funding alumni to attend foreign
accelerators. **[C]**

**2021 (17th):** Elginkan grand prize split between two projects for the first time
(Biopols and Ddosify). **[C]**

**2022 (18th):** Demo Day in **December 2022**. ODTÜ TEKNOKENT GM Serdar Alemdar frames
the program inside the technopark's export goal — *"this year we aim to reach $1bn in
exports, targeting Turkey's centennial in 2023"* — and notes a **$6M early-stage tech
investment fund** created with the Turkey Development Fund (Türkiye Kalkınma Fonu).
Technopark exports grew $100M (2018) → $300M+ (2021) → $700M+ projected (2022). **[C]**

**2023 (19th):** Campaign hashtag **#Fascinate / #Etkile**. Ankara final **7 December 2023**.
Sectors on stage: carbon capture, satellite solar panels, education technology, applied AI,
IoT, new materials. **[R]**

**2024 (20th):** Campaign hashtag **#TakeMyBreathAway**. Demo Day **4 December 2024**;
YFYİ entrepreneurs also took the **Take Off** stage. **[C]**

**2025 (21st):** Campaign hashtag **#Fascinate** carried over on the site. Demo Day
**17 December 2025**; 10 startups pitched. A parallel **KALTEV YFYİ** edition ran with
ODTÜ Northern Cyprus Campus / KALTEV. Investment partners now include **ODTÜ70 GSYF**,
**Growth Circuit** and **Collective Spark**. **[C]**

## Venues and cadence

| Aspect | Pattern |
|---|---|
| Application window | Historically **March–May** (2013–2018), moved to **August** (2019), then **autumn** (Sept–Oct) for the December Demo Days (2023–2025) |
| Final / Demo Day month | **November** (2007–2015) → **October** (2016–2018) → **November** (2019) → **April, online** (2020 cohort) → **December** (2022–2025) |

| Public vote | *Halk Oylaması* prize, 2015–2018 (6,874 voters in 2017) |
