# 06 — Partners, Sponsors and the Prize Architecture

## The constant: Elginkan Vakfı

**Elginkan Vakfı** (Elginkan Foundation, part of the Elginkan Group, İstanbul) has been the
**main sponsor since the very first edition in 2005** — 21 consecutive years. It maintains
its own public archive of YFYİ editions at
`elginkanvakfi.org.tr/odtu-yeni-fikirler-yeni-isler-yarisma/<year>` (pages exist for
2007–2018, 2020, 2021).

Elginkan runs a broader university project-competition portfolio, of which YFYİ is the
oldest and longest-lived strand:

| Program | Years supported |
|---|---|
| **ODTÜ Yeni Fikirler Yeni İşler** | 2005 → present |
| BÜ "İşte Bu Proje" Yarışması (Boğaziçi) | 2008–2014 |
| İTÜ Çekirdek Proje Yarışması | 2011–2014 |
| YTÜ Yıldızlı Projeler Yarışması | 2013–2014 |

Elginkan awards two things at YFYİ: the **Büyük Ödül** (grand cash prize, paid in
instalments tied to reaching production) and the **Uluslararasılaşma Ödülü /
Uluslararası Program Katılım Ödülü** (covers the cost of a foreign incubation/accelerator
program).

## Prize architecture: how the money is shaped

### Cash prize scale by era

| Era | Typical grand prize | Typical special award | Advertised total pool |
|---|---|---|---|
| 2007 | 50,000 TL | — | — |
| 2008–2011 | 75,000 TL | 10,000–25,000 TL | — |
| 2012–2013 | 100,000 TL ×3 grands | 25,000 TL | 400,000 TL cash / **1.5M TL** total support |
| 2014 | 100,000 TL ×4 grands | 25,000 TL ×8 US camps | **~2M TL** |
| 2015 | 100,000 TL + category grands | 25,000 TL US camps + $120k cloud credits | **~2M TL** |
| 2016 | 100,000 TL | 25,000–100,000 TL across 10 categories | — |
| 2017–2018 | 100,000 TL | 15,000–50,000 TL | — |
| 2019 | 50,000 TL ×2 | 15,000–50,000 TL | — |
| 2020–2022 | 100,000 TL | 30,000–50,000 TL | — |

### Non-cash instruments

- **US Camp** — 2 weeks (2015) or 4 weeks (2016) at T-Jump San Francisco
- **Draper University Hero Training** — 7 weeks (2017–2018)
- **Internationalisation Award** — Elginkan (foreign incubation costs) and ODTÜ TEKNOKENT
  (accelerator of the winner's choosing, plus mentoring/training)
- **Incubation Award** — free office at ODTÜ TEKNOKENT Incubation Centre (1–3 years
  depending on era); OSTİM Teknopark also gives an incubation award
- **Advertising support** — Onedio, up to 50,000 TL value
- **Cloud credits** — Microsoft Azure and IBM Bluemix, $120,000 each (2015)
- **Prototyping support** — Btech (2017)
- **Logistics/commerce** — PTT Kargo discount (40% for 1 year), PTTAVM shopping package
- **Technology package** — KoçSistem
- **Investment** — Growth Circuit direct investment; ODTÜ70 GSYF; Collective Spark;
  a **$6M early-stage technology fund** with the Türkiye Kalkınma Fonu (announced 2022)
- **Public vote (Halk Oylaması)** prize — 2,500–7,500 TL, 2015–2018
