## Positioning

YFYİ describes itself as **"Türkiye'nin öncü erken aşama hızlandırma programı"** — Turkey's
leading/first early-stage acceleration program. Three claims recur in official copy:

1. **First**: the earliest structured university-linked startup program in Turkey (2005),
   predating the modern Turkish accelerator wave by roughly a decade.
2. **Longest-running**: 21 consecutive editions.
3. **Full-funnel**: it takes people from *"I have an idea"* to *"I have a company with a
   first customer and an investor conversation"*, and keeps supporting alumni afterwards
   ("Being a YFYI member is gaining a life long support").

## What a participant actually gets

From the official site and program announcements: **[C]**

- Structured training + intensive mentoring (a strong mentor pool; 120+ mentors cumulatively)
- Working space at **ODTÜ TEKNOKENT** and at **CoZone** — described as the only co-working
  space in Turkey backed by a technopark
- Access to ODTÜ TEKNOKENT's global business network and investor networks
- Cash / in-kind awards from partner institutions at Demo Day
- Free office in the ODTÜ TEKNOKENT Incubation Centre for successful graduates
  (1 year free office cited in 2018; 2 years cited in 2013; 3 years in the early editions)
- Seed capital from YFYİ's partner institutions
- An "idea test environment" — a route to the first customer
- Internationalisation support (see `06-partners-sponsors-prizes.md`)

## One-line summary

> YFYİ started in 2005 as an ODTÜ student business-idea competition with a single grand
> prize; over 21 years it turned into a multi-stage, sponsor-funded, globally-linked
> accelerator that has seeded 200–250+ technology companies inside the ODTÜ TEKNOKENT
> ecosystem.
