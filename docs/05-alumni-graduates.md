## Best-documented alumni

### V-Count — cohort 2005
Retail people-counting and in-store analytics hardware/software.
- Ranked **9th among Turkey's Top 100 new-generation companies** (2018)
- Exports to **100+ countries**
- Offices in **Miami, Dubai, London, Hong Kong, Brussels**
- Now listed as a **YFYİ graduate business partner** on yfyi.com — i.e. it went from
  participant to sponsor. Website: tr.v-count.com

### Mobilus / Invidyo — cohort 2005
Started as **RotaMobil** in the 2005 edition; the team later ran **Invidyo** through the
Teknojumpp accelerator.
- Invidyo: AI-based image-processing to measure quality of care for home-care patients, the
  elderly and children
- Retail distribution in Turkey (E-Bebek, Babymall), **60 Toys "R" Us locations in Canada**,
  and Amazon online sales

### Kade Vision — cohort 2005
Received **10,000 TL seed funding** from YFYİ — one of the earliest recorded YFYİ cheques.

### ISSD — cohort 2010
Intelligent transportation systems; developer of the **Dynamic Junction Control System
(CHAOS®)**.
- **First place in two categories** at YFYİ 2010
- **International Intertraffic Best Project Award**, 2012
- Projected **35 million TL revenue in 2019**; annual growth 15–120%
- Later became a **YFYİ sponsor** (ISSD funded the 25,000 TL US Camp Special Award in 2015)
  and is listed among YFYİ graduate business partners. Website: issd.com.tr

### Alictus — cohort 2013
Hyper-casual game developer with its own analytics and publishing tools.
- 14-person team (2019), **10M+ monthly players**, mostly US
- **99% of revenue from exports**

### İnofab / Spirohome — cohorts 2014–2015
Wireless spirometer for respiratory patients (entered YFYİ PRO Health 2015).
- **€220K** raised in 2017 (500 Startups)
- **€1.1M** raised in 2018 (ACT + Growth Circuit)
- **CE certified** in 2019; expanded to **17 countries in 3 months**; sells direct and via
  European Amazon stores

### Btech Bilişim — cohort 2015
Won the **TEB Grand Prize** in the PRO Health category (spinal fusion screw guide system).
- Revenue doubled annually; passed **1 million TL** by year three
- 15+ employees by 2019; planning international expansion
- Later became a YFYİ sponsor itself — funded the **Btech Prototyping Special Award** in 2017

### Otsimo — cohort 2016
Special-education / autism learning app for children. Won the ODTÜ TEKNOKENT Special Award
and Onedio Special Award in 2016. Subsequently one of the better-known Turkish edtech
exports.

### Deplike — cohort 2016
Guitar-amp-simulation and music apps. ODTÜ TEKNOKENT Special Award + Onedio Special Award 2016.

### Akıllıfon — cohort 2019
AI-based robo-advisory and portfolio optimisation. Won the 30,000 TL KoçSistem Technology
Package Special Award at YFYİ 2019, then raised **600,000 TL from Lima Ventures**
(December 2020) — a directly traceable YFYİ → seed-round path.

### Ddosify — cohort 2021
High-performance distributed load-testing software; co-winner of the Elginkan Grand Prize.
Became a visible open-source performance-testing project internationally.

### Sensgreen / Blok-Z / Warden Legal / Viliks — cohort 2018
The four teams that took **Growth Circuit investment** during the 2018 program and went to
**Draper University Hero Training**. Sensgreen (IoT indoor air quality) also won the
25,000 TL Tüpraş award.

### Enhencer, FineDine Menu, Kuantek, Webgazer, Niceveri, Pyronome — 2017 orbit
These appeared in the 2017 T-JUMP Go Global / Draper cohorts around YFYİ. **FineDine**
(restaurant menu management) and **Enhencer** (e-commerce AI) both became internationally
selling Turkish SaaS companies.
