# 04 — Winners and Awards, Year by Year

The **Elginkan Vakfı Büyük Ödülü** (Elginkan Foundation Grand Prize) is the flagship award
every year. Its value went 50,000 TL (2007) → 75,000 TL (2008–2011) → **100,000 TL
(2012 onwards)**.
