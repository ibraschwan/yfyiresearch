# 07 — Applying to YFYİ, and the Wider Resource Map

## Who can apply

Eligibility has been stable in shape since 2015: **two profiles**.

### Profile A — idea stage (historically **YFYİ IDEA** / **T-IDEA**)
- Undergraduate, master's or PhD students
- Graduates **up to 5 years** since graduation
- No company yet; a technology-based business idea
- Route into **T-BİGG** if TÜBİTAK BİGG criteria are met → **150,000 TL** capital support

### Profile B — early-stage company (historically **YFYİ PRO** / **T-START**)
- Technology companies aged **0–3** (2015) or **0–4** (2016 onwards)
- Historically capped at companies that had raised **< 100,000 TL**
- Focus areas at various times: IT/computing, health, energy, durables & consumer
  electronics, defence
- **T-BOOST** (2016) targeted companies with product + team ready for foreign markets

Current public copy is broader: *"If you trust your idea but don't know where to start, or
if you have a tech startup and need support in commercialisation, YFYI can help."*

## Where YFYİ sits in the ODTÜ TEKNOKENT stack

YFYİ is the acceleration layer of a larger apparatus. The adjacent programs and entities
matter because YFYİ graduates flow into them.

| Entity | What it is |
|---|---|
| **ODTÜ TEKNOKENT** | Turkey's flagship technopark, operating since 2001. Pre-incubation, incubation, business development, investment and acceleration programs. 330+ companies (2017). |
| **YFYİ** | The early-stage acceleration program (this dossier). |
| **ATOM** | ODTÜ TEKNOKENT pre-incubation / entrepreneurship program. |
| **BİGG** | TÜBİTAK's 1512 individual young entrepreneur support, run through ODTÜ TEKNOKENT (T-BİGG); 150,000 TL scale. |
| **Growth Circuit** | ODTÜ TEKNOKENT's San Francisco-based acceleration + investment company, founded 2016. Invests directly in YFYİ cohorts. https://growthcircuit.co |
| **T-Jump** | ODTÜ TEKNOKENT's earlier San Francisco acceleration centre (2014–2017); US Camps and Go Global. |
| **CoZone** | Co-working space; billed as the only co-working space in Turkey backed by a technopark. YFYİ participants get access. |
| **ODTÜ70 GSYF** | ODTÜ 70th-anniversary Venture Capital Investment Fund. YFYİ investment partner. https://www.odtu70.vc |
| **ASO TEKNOPARK** | Ankara Chamber of Industry technopark, an ODTÜ TEKNOKENT affiliate. |
| **T.ICT / TSSK / T.HEALTH** | Sector clusters (ICT, defence & aviation, health). |
| **ODTÜ TTO** | Technology transfer office. |
| **Take Off** (TEKNOFEST) | National startup summit; YFYİ 2024 entrepreneurs took the Take Off stage. |
| **KALTEV** | Partner institution for the 2025 ODTÜ Northern Cyprus Campus edition. |
