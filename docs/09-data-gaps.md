# 09 — Data Gaps, Conflicts and Confidence Notes

Read this before quoting any number from this dossier.

---

## 1. Edition numbering: a one-year offset between two official sources

**The conflict.** ODTÜ / ODTÜ TEKNOKENT numbers 2005 as the 1st edition, which makes:
2019 = 15th, 2020 = 16th, 2021 = 17th … 2025 = 21st. This is internally consistent with
every ODTÜ TEKNOKENT press release located, and with yfyi.com's own "21. sene" claim in 2025.

**Elginkan Vakfı's archive disagrees for the last two pages it published:** its `/2020`
page says *"15'incisi"* and its `/2021` page says *"16'ıncısı"*. But its `/2015` = 11th,
`/2016` = 12th, `/2017` = 13th and `/2018` = 14th all match ODTÜ's numbering. The 2019
edition simply has no Elginkan archive page, and it looks like the numbering slipped by one
from that point.

**This dossier uses the ODTÜ numbering.** Also note Elginkan's own `/2010` page calls that
edition "beşincisi" (5th) when it is the 6th — a second, unrelated typo in that archive.

## 2. Application counts do not reconcile

| Claim | Source | Year |
|---|---|---|
| "more than **8,000** applications in 12 years" | Rector Mustafa Verşan Kök | 2016 |
| "**3,500+** applications this year" | ODTÜ TEKNOKENT | 2016 |
| "**1,500+** applications this year" | Webrazzi | 2016 |
| "**7,000+** projects evaluated in 16 years" | Webrazzi | 2021 |
| "**5,500+** applications" (cumulative) | yfyi.com | 2025 |

These cannot all be true on the same counting basis. Most likely explanations: (a) some
counts are *entrepreneurs*, others *business ideas*, others *teams* — the 2013 and 2014
figures explicitly distinguish these; (b) the 2016 3,500 vs 1,500 gap is a raw-registration
vs completed-application distinction; (c) the current 5,500 figure appears to be a
re-baselined count covering a narrower definition. **Treat the 5,500+/230+/120+/25+ block
as marketing figures, not audited data.**

## 4. Overlapping events on 4 December 2024

YFYİ/ODTÜ TEKNOKENT channels confirm the **4 December 2024** Demo Day and a broader award
list including Raw Banana Studio, Agada Tech, Datablast, Umigame Studios, Vignetim,
Toprak Mekatronik, Alter, DATASCOPE.AI, Viseur AI, STEP4, HyperCrops, Meetmeat
Biotechnology, Posamas and Hyperia. On the same day at the same venue (ODTÜ Kültür ve Kongre
Merkezi), the **Deep Tech Ankara** accelerator (Ankara Development Agency) held its own
Demo Day with 17 startups, whose winners included **Hypercrops (Ostim Teknokent incubation
award)**, Vignetim, Datascope, Viseur AI, Hyperia and Meetmeat.

Either the two programs were co-located and shared an award ceremony, or one startup won
similar awards at both. **Do not merge the two award lists without further verification.**

## 5. 2013 finalist arithmetic

The Webrazzi report says 11 finalists competed, then breaks them down as 5 (Genel) + 4
(Bilişim ve Telekom) + 4 (Savunma Sanayii) = 13. One of the two figures is wrong in the
source. The "1% of applicants reached the final" framing in the same article is consistent
with ~11.

## 6. 2014 vs 2015 application figures look swapped

Webrazzi (2014): "1,353 business idea applications, of which 1,244 passed the first stage."
Elginkan Vakfı (2015): "1,243 entrepreneurs applied with 1,353 business ideas."
The near-identical 1,353 and 1,244/1,243 pairs across two different years suggest one
source reused the other's numbers. Both are recorded here as published. **[?]**

## 7. 2018 Demo Day: date and cohort size

- Date: Webrazzi and the event announcement say **6 October 2018**; Habertürk's report is
  dated 8–9 October 2018. Used here: **6 October 2018**.
- Startups presenting: the selection article says **21 teams** selected on 5 September;
  the results coverage says **22 startup projects** presented. Both are recorded.

## 8. "YFYİ Mezunu İş Ortakları" tier is not a verified alumni list

yfyi.com groups **V-Count, ISSD, BiTES, Enocta, ICterra, VLMedia** under "YFYİ Graduate
Business Partners." V-Count (2005) and ISSD (2010) are independently confirmed as YFYİ
cohort members. For BiTES, Enocta, ICterra and VLMedia, no source was found naming a YFYİ
cohort year — they may be ODTÜ TEKNOKENT ecosystem graduates rather than YFYİ program
graduates. **[?]**

## 9. No public alumni database

There is no official, complete list of the "230+ startups". This dossier reconstructs
roughly **150 named startups** across the editions where cohort lists were published
(2015, 2017, 2018, 2019, 2020, and partial lists for 2021–2025). Cohort lists for
**2005–2014, 2016, 2022, 2023 and 2024** are largely unpublished. Anyone needing the
complete roster should request it directly from ODTÜ TEKNOKENT
(info@odtuteknokent.com.tr) or the YFYİ team.

## 10. Post-program outcome data is thin

Only ~12 alumni have documented post-program outcomes (funding, revenue, exits). No exit
or acquisition data was found for any YFYİ graduate. Revenue and headcount figures for
Alictus, ISSD, Btech and Spirohome are as of **2019** and are now seven years stale.

## 11. Currency and inflation

All prize amounts are nominal Turkish Lira at the time of award. A 100,000 TL grand prize
in 2012 and a 100,000 TL grand prize in 2022 are not remotely comparable in real terms —
the prize was *not* indexed. Any analysis of "prize generosity over time" must deflate,
and should note that the flagship prize has been held nominally flat at 100,000 TL since
2012 (and was split between two winners in 2019, 2021 and 2022).

## 12. Recent-year coverage is weaker than mid-decade coverage

Turkish tech press covered YFYİ intensively 2013–2021 (Webrazzi, egirişim). Coverage
thinned after 2021, and 2023–2025 information comes largely from YFYİ's own social channels
and university relay announcements. Winner lists for the last three editions are therefore
partial and should be treated as **incomplete rather than complete**.
