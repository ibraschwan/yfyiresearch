# 03 — Editions Year by Year: How Each Was Conducted

Edition numbering below follows the **ODTÜ / ODTÜ TEKNOKENT** convention (2005 = 1st).
Note that elginkanvakfi.org.tr's archive pages for 2020 and 2021 label those editions
"15th" and "16th" respectively — one lower than the organiser's own numbering. See
`09-data-gaps.md`. **[?]**

---

## 2005 — 1st edition
- **Format:** Student business-idea competition, launched by ODTÜ under the philosophy of
  producing *"iş bulan değil, iş kuran mezun"* — graduates who found businesses rather than
  find jobs (Prof. Dr. Mustafa Verşan Kök). **[C]**
- **Applications:** **195 entrepreneurs** — the smallest cohort in program history. **[C]**
- **Main sponsor:** Elginkan Vakfı, from this first edition onwards. **[C]**
- **Notable outcomes:** Three companies were founded out of the 2005 edition. Alumni
  traceable to 2005 include **V-Count**, **Mobilus** (RotaMobil) and **Kade Vision**
  (which received 10,000 TL seed from YFYİ). **[R]**

## 2006 — 2nd edition
- Elginkan Vakfı continued as main sponsor. One company founded from this edition. **[?]**
- **Final:** **11 November 2006**, ODTÜ Kültür ve Kongre Merkezi. Contemporary SisoNews
  coverage names **4 finalist groups**: **PALMAX** (*Oyna ve Öğren*), **RİMS** (*Dual Sim
  Kart Cep Telefonu*), **ANKA** (*Presentation Tool*) and **Köprü** (*Hayatı Yeniden
  Okumak*). **PALMAX** won the first prize: **50,000 YTL** founding capital plus **3 years
  free ODTÜ TEKNOKENT office space**. **Köprü** received a **10,000 YTL jury special
  award**. **[R]**

## 2007 — 3rd edition
- **Final:** **17 November 2007**
- **Format:** *"En İyi Teknolojik İş Fikri"* (Best Technological Business Idea) — **4
  finalist teams**.
- **Winner:** **JeoTim** — project **PetÇed**. Team: Aslı İlbay (ODTÜ Geological
  Engineering, 4th year) and Mehmet Erdem Hamamcı (3rd year). Geochemistry-based sampling
  apparatus ("AEB" and "AET" types, patents filed) for energy-raw-material exploration and
  Environmental Impact Assessment; chromatographic analysis data modelled in 3D. Solid-phase
  and gas-phase samplers reaching 1.5 m depth; used for heavy-metal pollution mapping, oil
  exploration, and leak detection on gas/oil pipelines and underground storage.
- **Prize:** **50,000 TL** first prize, paid in instalments tied to reaching production. **[C]**

## 2008 — 4th edition
- **Final:** **15 November 2008** — 4 finalist teams.
- **Winner:** **Biyonesil** — *Biyonik Mayın Tespit Sistemi* (Bionic Landmine Detection
  System). Genetically modified micro-organisms sprayed from aircraft make buried
  landmines luminesce visibly. Team of four young women.
- **Prizes:** **75,000 TL** Elginkan Vakfı Technology Award + **50,000 TL** Savunma Sanayii
  Müsteşarlığı (Defence Industries Undersecretariat) Special Award + the right to operate
  as a company inside ODTÜ TEKNOKENT for 3 years. **[C]**

    vehicles; target discrimination, projectile angle-of-arrival, MANPAD detection; civil

    Prize + 25,000 TL DenizBank Special + 25,000 TL Aselsan Special**.
  - **Javatar** — alternative communication device for speech-impaired users, advanced
    text-to-speech. **75,000 TL Türk Telekom Grand Prize**.

  - **PragmaCraft** — intelligent search assistant. 10,000 TL Türk Telekom 2nd prize +
    **25,000 TL Intel Special Award**.

## 2014 — 10th edition
- **Run with:** Teknopark İstanbul; **TEB** as organisation main sponsor.
- **Applications:** **1,353 business ideas**; 1,244 passed stage 1; **47** reached the final
  stage; **21 teams** competed in **5 categories** (Genel, Bilişim & Telekom, Savunma
  Sanayii, Enerji, Sağlık). Applications closed 28 April 2014; 60 ideas passed pre-selection.
- **Final:** **8 November 2014**, ODTÜ Kültür ve Kongre Merkezi (Kemal Kurdaş Salonu).
  Jury drawn from academics, investors, company owners, experienced founders, financial
  institutions and public bodies.
- **Total award value advertised:** ~**2 million TL** (685,000 TL cash in the March
  announcement, expanded by the September announcement).
- **Prize schedule:** Elginkan Vakfı Grand 100k · SSM Grand 100k · General Electric Grand
  100k · Bilişim ve Telekomünikasyon Grand 100k · **US Camp special prizes of 25k each**
  from Arçelik, Aselsan, General Electric, Microsoft, OSTİM, TEB, YEDAŞ, Teknopark İstanbul.
- **Winner:** **2C** (Ceren Can, Pelin Koçak, Pelin Şermikli) — **AFB KİT**, an agricultural
  diagnostic/treatment kit for American Foulbrood disease in bees, high export value.
  **100,000 TL Elginkan Vakfı Grand Prize** + **25,000 TL OSTİM US Camp Special Award**. **[C]**
- **Support mechanics:** teams reaching stage 2 got 3 months in the incubation centre;
  teams pitching in the final got 1–2 years in the incubation centre.
- Also from this year: **Spirohome / İnofab** cohort 2014–2015. **[R]**

## 2016 — 12th edition
- **Renaming:** officially an **acceleration program**, not a competition.
- **Applications:** reported as **3,500+** by ODTÜ TEKNOKENT and as **1,500+** by Webrazzi
  — sources conflict. **[?]** Cumulative applications stated as 8,000+ over 12 years.
- **Structure:** T-IDEA / T-START / T-BOOST, plus T-BİGG (TÜBİTAK BİGG route, 150,000 TL).
- **Bootcamp:** run by **String Ventures**, 21–27 June 2016.
- **Final:** **15 October 2016**, ODTÜ Kemal Kurdaş Salonu. **20+ projects**, **10 award
  categories**, prizes from **25,000 to 100,000 TL**.
- **US camp:** all winning teams earned a **4-week camp at T-Jump SF**.
- **Grand prize:** **Isıteks** — silver-nanowire-coated smart textile that heats under DC
  current to protect the body from cold. 100,000 TL Elginkan Vakfı Grand Prize. **[C]**
- Quote — Rector Kök: *"In this 12-year journey we have received more than 8,000
  applications."* Quote — GM Kızıltaş: *"In these 12 years more than 200 projects
  incorporated and joined the economy, and over 1,000 people were employed."*

## 2017 — 13th edition
- **Applications:** **400+** → **50 teams** into the pre-accelerator.
- **Pre-accelerator:** 6 weeks, **UC Berkeley Innovation Acceleration Group** + **T-JUMP**,
  Lean Launch method + Design Thinking module. UC Berkeley worked with 70+ entrepreneurs
  on 50+ ideas across two visits to ODTÜ TEKNOKENT.
- **Bootcamp:** 26–27 May 2017 at ODTÜ TEKNOKENT.
- **US leg:** **7 teams** to **Draper University Hero Training**, 7 weeks in San Francisco
  (icaked, Restearn, Peerket, Seyis.co, Trendoline, plus Lingo and Webgazer via T-BİGG),
  finishing with pitches to **Tim Draper**.
- **Also:** a **T-JUMP Go Global** cohort (Bambulabs, FineDine Menu, Niceveri, Kuantek,
  Smarteng, Pyronome, Dijifin, Drivebuddy, Gauss, Enhencer, Mavilab/Cybelle Lazer).
- **Demo Day:** **14 October 2017**, ODTÜ Kültür ve Kongre Merkezi, 09:00. **22 startups**,
  3-minute pitches, hosted by **Metin Uca**. Public vote drew **6,874 voters**.
- **Investment:** **Growth Circuit** selected 7 startups for investment.
- **Context stat:** 330+ technology companies inside ODTÜ TEKNOKENT, 60%+ founded there;
  20% founded by academics; $1.2bn technology export revenue. **[C]**
